## General

**Original:** The *General* tab enables you to edit the following details for your account profile:

- Details: to see the name associated with your account.
- Connected accounts: to manage Google, GitHub, GitLab and email accounts connected with your Strapi Cloud account.
- Delete account: to permanently delete your Strapi Cloud account.

**中文译文:** *General* 标签页可以管理以下账户信息：

- Details：查看与账户关联的姓名；
- Connected accounts：管理与 Strapi Cloud 账户连接的 Google、GitHub、GitLab 和 email 账户；
- Delete account：永久删除 Strapi Cloud 账户。

### Managing connected accounts

**Original:** You can connect a Google, GitLab, GitHub and email account to your Strapi Cloud account. The _Connected accounts_ section lists accounts that are currently connected to your Strapi Cloud account. From there you can also connect a new Google, GitLab, GitHub and email account if one is not already connected.

**中文译文:** 你可以将 Google、GitLab、GitHub 和 email 账户连接到 Strapi Cloud 账户。*Connected accounts* 区域会列出当前已连接的账户；如果某类账户尚未连接，也可以从这里新增连接。

**Original:** To connect a new Google, GitLab, GitHub or email account to your Strapi Cloud account, click on the **Connect account** button and follow the next steps on the corresponding website.

**中文译文:** 若要连接新的 Google、GitLab、GitHub 或 email 账户，点击 **Connect account**，然后按照对应网站上的后续步骤完成授权。

**Original:** You can also click on the three dots button of a connected account and click on the "Manage on" button to manage your GitHub, GitLab or Google account directly on the corresponding website.

**中文译文:** 对于已经连接的账户，还可以点击其三点菜单，然后选择 **Manage on**，直接前往对应网站管理 GitHub、GitLab 或 Google 账户。

### Deleting Strapi Cloud account

**Original:** You can delete your Strapi Cloud account, but it will be permanent and irreversible. All associated projects and their data will be deleted as well and the subscriptions for the projects will automatically be canceled.

**中文译文:** 你可以删除 Strapi Cloud 账户，但此操作是永久且不可逆的。所有关联项目及其数据也会被删除，同时项目订阅会自动取消。

**Original:** 1. In the *Delete account* section of the *General* tab, click on the **Delete account** button.

**中文译文:** 1. 在 *General* 标签页的 *Delete account* 区域点击 **Delete account**。

**Original:** 2. In the dialog, type `DELETE` in the textbox.

**中文译文:** 2. 在确认对话框的文本框中输入 `DELETE`。

**Original:** 3. Confirm the deletion of your account by clicking on the **Delete** button.

**中文译文:** 3. 点击 **Delete**，确认删除账户。
