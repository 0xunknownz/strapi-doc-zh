**Original:** Strapi AI includes 1,000 credits per month on Growth, and 10 free credits during the free trial. Strapi AI is not available on Enterprise plans.

**中文译文:** Strapi AI 在 Growth plan 中每月包含 1,000 credits；free trial 提供 10 free credits。Enterprise plan 当前不包含 Strapi AI。

**Original:** Lightweight actions consume fewer credits; complex actions consume more.

**中文译文:** Lightweight AI action 消耗较少 credits，复杂任务消耗更多。

**Original:** Credit usage is available in Settings Overview. Notifications are sent at 80%, 90%, and 100%.

**中文译文:** 可在 admin panel 的 Settings Overview 查看 credit usage；使用量达到 80%、90%、100% 时会发送提醒。

**Original:** Credits are shared across all users in the same project instance. Overages are billed monthly.

**中文译文:** 同一 project instance 的 users 共享 credits。Credits 用完后仍可继续使用，overage 按月计费。
