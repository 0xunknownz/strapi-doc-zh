# 📖 对照翻译：Invoice statuses

> Source: `docusaurus/docs/snippets/invoices-statuses.md`  
> Upstream SHA: `e97a690a10d384d4b611a44286a10a2834069f63`

**Original:** Invoices can have any of the following statuses:

**中文译文:** Invoice 可能处于以下任一状态：

**Original:**
- Paid: the payment has been done and the invoice is available, no additional action is required.
- Payment pending: the invoice is not complete or validated yet.
- Payment due: the payment didn't go through and needs to be fixed.
- Not paid: the payment has failed and won't automatically be retried.
- Voided: the invoice has been cancelled.

**中文译文:**
- Paid：付款已经完成，invoice 可用，无需执行其他操作；
- Payment pending：invoice 尚未完成或尚未验证；
- Payment due：付款未成功，需要处理付款问题；
- Not paid：付款失败，并且不会自动重试；
- Voided：invoice 已取消。

**Original:** Click the download icon to download an invoice.

**中文译文:** 点击 download 图标即可下载 invoice。
